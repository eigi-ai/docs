---
title: "Connect a messaging account"
description: "Reach Eigi from your phone or team chat and choose who answers."
---

In Desktop, open **Settings → Channels**, choose the service you want to use, and follow its connection steps. Keep Eigi running on the computer that owns the connection.

| Service | What to connect | How to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | Open Linked devices on your phone and scan Eigi’s QR code. | In the default self-chat mode, send a message in **Message yourself**. |
| Telegram | Provide the bot token from BotFather, then follow Eigi’s owner verification prompt. | Open the bot in Telegram and send it a direct message. |
| Slack | Connect the Slack app with the bot and app tokens requested in setup. Confirm the workspace and the owner. | Send the bot a DM or mention it in a channel it has joined. |
| Discord | Supply the bot token, add the bot to your server, and confirm the owner. | DM the bot or mention it in the selected server. |

## Check who can talk to it

On the channel’s settings page, review the rules for direct messages and groups. Let one person test access before opening it to a larger group. For WhatsApp, **Who Eigi answers** lets you switch from self-chat to replying to other people on a separate bot number.

**Who answers** controls whether an allowed conversation goes to Main or to a Buddy. For a single conversation, have the person message the bot first, then [route that conversation to a Buddy](/eigi-desktop/buddies#send-a-channel-chat-to-this-buddy).

## When the bot is connected but silent

- Check **Run on this computer**. A bot runs in one Eigi at a time. If the bot runs elsewhere, open that Eigi or use the channel’s controls to move it.
- Check the owner verification step, the allowed people or groups, and any rule requiring a mention.
- For Slack or Discord, use the connection page to check the bot’s permissions. Fix missing permissions in the service, then select **Check again**.
- If WhatsApp is waiting for a scan, reopen Linked devices and scan the current code.
