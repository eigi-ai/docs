title: "Use a desktop app with Eigi"
description: "Let Eigi work in an app window, review an Appshot, and take back control."
---

## Ask Eigi to work in an app

1. Open **Settings → Computer use** and turn on **Use apps on this computer**. Complete the access prompts shown for your operating system. On macOS, quit and reopen Eigi if you have just granted screen capture.
2. Open a blank document in a text editor. In a Desktop chat, ask “In my text editor, type a three-item packing list and leave it open for me to review.”
3. When Eigi asks to use the editor, check which app it names and choose how long to allow access. Follow its steps in chat and inspect the result in the editor.
4. To stop app control from anywhere, press **Control + Option + Command + .** on macOS or **Ctrl + Alt + .** on Windows and Ubuntu. You can also choose **Stop** from the Eigi menu bar or tray.

Under **Computer use**, review the always-allowed and never-allowed apps. **Pause while you use the same app** lets you take over temporarily by clicking or typing. **Scheduled tasks may use always-allowed apps** controls access for unattended work.

## Ask about a window with an Appshot

An Appshot attaches a window to a draft. Use one when you want an explanation of an error or help understanding an unfamiliar screen.

1. Open **Settings → Appshots**. Check the shortcut and whether **Include app text** is on.
2. Bring the window you want to ask about forward and use the shortcut. Review the captured image and the text the app made available.
3. Add a question, such as “Which field is causing this form error?”, then send the draft.

## Work with a website or service

For a website task, choose the browser setup under **Settings → Browser and extension**. Ask Eigi to locate a specific page or read an item, and follow the browser activity in chat. For account data available through a connector, authorize the service under **Settings → Plugins → Connectors**. To give a particular Buddy access to that service, open its [Buddy settings](/eigi-desktop/buddies#give-the-buddy-the-right-access).
