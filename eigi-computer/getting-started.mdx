Eigi Computer runs your agent in the cloud. Its files, chats and connected channels live there. You can close your browser while the computer continues to work.

## Start the computer

1. Sign in and open [Eigi Computer in Studio](https://studio.eigi.ai/eigi-agent). Start a chat to open **Set up your Eigi computer**.
2. Pick an available size and review its specifications, monthly price, and renewal terms. Studio shows the charge against your Eigi balance before you confirm.
3. Select **Switch on** and wait for the computer to become ready. You can check its state under **Settings → Computer**.
4. In chat, ask “Create a file called first-note.md with three things you can help me do.” Open the file in the workspace and ask Eigi to add a fourth item. You now have a chat and a file you can return to from Studio.

## Work while you are away

Open **Settings → Channels** to connect a messaging account, then send a test message from that app. For a repeating task, try “Every Monday at 9 AM in my time zone, remind me to review this note.” Review the schedule and where it delivers the reminder. [Connect a channel](/eigi-computer/channels) · [Set up an automation](/eigi-computer/automation).

## Manage power and billing

| Action in Settings → Computer | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Pause computer** | Work stops. Billing continues while paused. Choose **Start computer** to resume. |
| **Restart** | Restarts a running computer. Allow time for it to become ready before retrying a task. |
| **Shut down** | Studio backs up files and stops billing when shutdown completes. Unused paid time is saved for this computer size. |
| **Restore your computer** | Studio restores the backup to the original size and shows how saved time applies before you confirm. |

The Computer page shows the state, renewal, saved time, and last backup even when the computer is off. If a channel or scheduled job goes quiet, check this page before changing its configuration.

For work on files that live on your laptop, [start with Desktop](/eigi-desktop/getting-started). To call the cloud computer from software, [enable the Developer API](/eigi-agent/developer-api).
