With a channel connected to the cloud computer, you can message Eigi while the computer is running. For a bot that runs on your laptop, use the [Desktop channel guide](/eigi-desktop/messaging).

1. Open **Settings → Channels** on your running Eigi Computer. Choose the service and follow its setup steps.
2. Complete the account or bot verification. Check the account name, where the bot runs, and the rules for who can message it.
3. Send a simple question from the connected app. Open **Show chats** in Studio to find the conversation and confirm who answered.

| Channel | First test |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | Scan the link code with your phone and send a message in the chat allowed by your WhatsApp settings. |
| Telegram | Connect a bot and verify its owner, then send a DM to the bot. |
| Slack | Connect the workspace app, then DM it or mention it in a channel it has joined. |
| Discord | Add the bot to the intended server, verify ownership, and send a DM or mention. |
| Instagram | Complete Instagram authorization with the professional account requested in setup, then test an incoming DM. |

## Choose who answers

Review the channel’s direct-message and group rules before adding other people. If a group requires a mention, mention the bot in your test. A routing choice sends an allowed conversation to Main or a particular Buddy. Check the Buddy is active and which computer currently runs the bot if a reply comes from the wrong place.

Scheduled jobs can deliver results to a connected channel. Check both the run and its delivery in [Automations](/eigi-computer/automation).
