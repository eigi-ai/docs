## Overview

Eigi Computer is your own personal AI that lives on its own dedicated computer — always on, always yours. Unlike a chatbot that forgets you after every conversation, your Eigi has its own workspace, its own memory, and its own tools. It can browse the web, work on files, run commands, connect to your apps, and reach you on the messaging platforms you already use.

Think of it as hiring a tireless personal assistant who has their own desk, their own computer, and never goes offline.

## What You Can Do With It

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Research and browsing" icon="globe">
    Ask Eigi to research anything online. It opens websites in its own browser,
    reads pages, compares sources, and gives you a summary — you can even watch
    it browse live from the chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Documents and files" icon="file-lines">
    Eigi can create, read, and edit files in its workspace — reports, notes,
    spreadsheets, code. You can browse its workspace, reference any file in
    chat, and download what it produces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Automations and reminders" icon="clock">
    Set up reminders and recurring tasks in plain language — "send me a summary
    of my inbox every morning at 9" — and Eigi runs them on schedule, on its
    own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connect your apps" icon="plug">
    Plug in tools like Gmail, Notion, GitHub, and hundreds of others through
    connectors. Once connected, Eigi can act in those apps on your behalf.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Personal assistant on chat apps" icon="mobile">
    Link your WhatsApp, Slack, Instagram, or Telegram and message Eigi like you
    message a friend. It can also send you proactive updates there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The Main Sections

Once your Eigi Computer is running, the dashboard gives you everything in one place:

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Talk to your Eigi, watch it work, manage sessions |
| **Automation** | Reminders, scheduled tasks, and webhooks |
| **Channels** | Connect WhatsApp, Slack, Instagram, Telegram |
| **Connectors** | Plug in external apps and tools (MCP) |
| **Plugins** | Add-on skill packs that extend what Eigi can do |
| **Memory** | See and manage what Eigi remembers about you |
| **Settings** | Instructions, permissions, skills, and personalization |
