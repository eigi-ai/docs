---
title: "What is Eigi Computer?"
description: "Use a personal cloud workspace for chat, files, channels, and scheduled work."
icon: "computer"
---

Eigi Computer is a private cloud workspace where Eigi Agent can research a question, work with files, or carry out a scheduled task. You reach it from Studio or through a connected messaging channel. While the cloud computer is running, it can keep working after you close your browser or turn off your laptop.

| Give your agent access to a service or reusable instructions | [Connectors](/eigi-computer/connectors) and [plugins](/eigi-computer/plugins) |
| Reach it from a chat app or trigger it on a schedule | [Channels](/eigi-computer/channels) and [automations](/eigi-computer/automation) |

| Use your cloud agent from an application | [Developer API](/eigi-agent/developer-api) |

To use your laptop’s files and applications directly, [install Eigi Desktop](/eigi-desktop/getting-started). For a physical board with a microphone and speaker, [set up Eigi Gadgets](/eigi-gadgets/getting-started).
