## Overview

Settings is where you shape your Eigi's personality, boundaries, and abilities. Everything is optional — Eigi works well out of the box — but a few minutes here makes it distinctly *yours*.

---

## Personalization

Tell Eigi who you are and how you like to work: your name, your role, your tone preferences, standing instructions ("always answer in bullet points", "my timezone is IST"). Eigi applies this to every conversation, on every channel.

<Tip>
  You can also just tell Eigi these things in chat — personalization and memory
  work together. The Personalization page is simply the place to see and edit
  it all directly.
</Tip>

## The Settings Pages

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="General" icon="gear">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Context" icon="file-lines">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Skills" icon="graduation-cap">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sub-agents" icon="users-gear">

    by connectors and plugins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Environment" icon="terminal">

    referenced securely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Memory" icon="brain">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Developer" icon="code">

  </Accordion>

## Permissions: How Independent Should Eigi Be?

This is the setting that matters most. Eigi can range from "ask me before anything" to "handle it and tell me after."

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Careful" icon="hand">
    Eigi asks before actions that change things — sending, deleting, buying,
    posting. Good while you're building trust.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Independent" icon="rocket">
    Eigi acts freely within the boundaries you've set and only checks in for
    genuinely sensitive steps. Good once your rules are dialed in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Either way, sensitive input (passwords, one-time codes) always goes through secure prompts — never plain chat text — and you can review everything Eigi did in the session history.

## People

The **People** section lists everyone Eigi knows from your conversations across channels. When the same person appears on WhatsApp and Slack, you can link those identities so Eigi treats them as one person — with one shared history.
